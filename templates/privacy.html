Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide you with our expense tracking services, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your email address and password used for authentication.
  • Financial Data: Transaction amounts, dates, categories, and descriptions that you manually input.
  • Usage Data: Basic information about how you interact with the app to help us improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Spendly service.
  • Generate spending summaries and analysis for your account.
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our platform.
  • Communicate with you regarding account-related notifications.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and financial information from unauthorized access. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently share your personal financial data with third-party services. If we introduce any third-party integrations in the future, we will clearly disclose them and seek your explicit consent.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
